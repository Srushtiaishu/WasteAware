Local Recycling Policies

Here are key local policies every resident should know to recycle responsibly:

Mandatory Segregation

Households must separate dry and wet waste.

Plastic Ban Zones

Certain areas prohibit single-use plastic bags and straws.

E-waste Drop-off

Electronics must be disposed of at approved collection points.

Green Rewards

Some municipalities offer points or discounts for consistent recycling.

Organic Waste Composting

Residents are encouraged to compost kitchen and garden waste at home or use community compost bins.

Bulk Waste Collection

Large items like furniture or appliances must be scheduled for special bulk waste collection days.

Why Every Action Counts

Improper waste management pollutes air and water, increases disease risks (including respiratory infections, diarrhea, and vector-borne diseases), and contributes 5% of global greenhouse gas emissions. Small changes at home separating waste, recycling clean materials, and composting organics have outsized benefits for community health, climate resilience, and environmental justice.
